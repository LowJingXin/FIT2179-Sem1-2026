01 — The Big Picture

A Nation of 34 Million

Malaysia's population grew 46% in 25 years — from 23.5 million in 2000 to 34.2 million in 2025. Growth briefly reversed in 2020, the clearest COVID-19 signal in the data, before resuming.

03 — Who We Are

A Multi-Ethnic Mosaic

Bumiputera Malays are the largest and fastest-growing group. The Chinese community has been stable since ~2015. Non-citizens dipped sharply in 2020–21 as borders closed. The dumbbell chart directly compares each group's size in 2000 vs 2025.

05 — Life & Death

Births Falling, Deaths Rising

National births fell 15% from 2000 to 2023. Deaths nearly doubled in 2021 — Malaysia's COVID-19 Delta wave. The natural increase (births minus deaths) keeps narrowing. Drag the sliders on the state charts to travel through time.
